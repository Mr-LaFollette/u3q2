How to type your answer

  • Write answers in a + bi form, like 3 − 2i. Use the letter i.
  • Fractions: type 3/5 + 4/5i or (3 + 4i)/5. Reduce every fraction.
  • Answers like −1, i, or 7i are fine when one part is zero.
  • No i in the denominator, no i², and no square roots of negatives in your final answer.
  • Give exact answers, not decimals.
